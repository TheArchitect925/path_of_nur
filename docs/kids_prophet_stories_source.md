## prophet_adam_bedtime_story_v1

- `id`: `prophet_adam_bedtime_story_v1`
- `prophet_key`: `adam`
- `audience`: `kids`
- `format`: `bedtime_story`
- `status`: `source_draft`
- `title`: `Prophet Adam – A Children’s Version`
- `quran_refs`:
  - `2:37`

### Story

Long, long ago, before there were cities, animals, or people, the world was quiet.

Allah created the heavens, the stars, the mountains, and the oceans. Everything was beautiful.

Then Allah decided to create something special.

From the earth itself, Allah created the very first human being.

His name was Adam.

Allah gave Adam knowledge and taught him the names of things. Adam was wise and curious. The angels were amazed by what Adam knew.

Allah honored Adam and gave him a beautiful place to live called Jannah — Paradise. It was filled with rivers, trees, sweet fruits, and peaceful animals.

But Adam was alone.

So Allah created a companion for him, Hawwa (Eve), so they would not feel lonely. Now Adam and Hawwa lived happily together in Paradise.

Allah told them something important.

They could enjoy everything in Paradise, except for one tree.

“Do not go near this tree,” Allah told them.

For a long time they stayed away from it.

But there was someone who did not like Adam.

His name was Iblis.

Iblis whispered to them and tried to trick them.

“Maybe the tree is special,” he said.

Adam and Hawwa became curious.

They forgot Allah’s warning and ate from the tree.

Suddenly they realized they had made a mistake.

They felt very sad and asked Allah to forgive them.

Allah is the Most Merciful, and He forgave them.

But it was time for Adam and Hawwa to leave Paradise and live on Earth.

Allah told them:

“On Earth you will live, learn, and build your lives. I will send guidance so people know the right path.”

Adam became the first prophet, teaching his children about Allah and how to live good lives.

And that is how the story of humanity began.

### Lesson For Kids

Everyone makes mistakes.

But the best people are the ones who say sorry, learn, and try again.

Allah loves those who turn back to Him.

### Qur'an Reference

“Then Adam received words from his Lord, and He accepted his repentance. Indeed, He is the Accepting of repentance, the Most Merciful.”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 2:37`

### Story

Long, long ago, many years after Prophet Adam lived on Earth, people began to forget something very important.

They forgot about Allah.

At first, people were kind and good. But slowly, they started doing wrong things. They stopped listening to the truth.

Allah chose a kind and patient man to guide them.

His name was Nuh.

Prophet Nuh loved his people. He wanted them to be safe and happy. So he gently told them:

“Worship Allah alone. Do good. Be kind.”

But many people did not listen.

Days passed…
Months passed…
Years passed…

Still, Prophet Nuh did not give up.

He kept reminding them, again and again, with patience and love.

But most people laughed at him.

They said, “Why should we listen to you?”

Even then, Prophet Nuh stayed strong.

Allah saw his patience.

Then Allah gave him a very special instruction.

“Build a great Ark (a big ship),” Allah told him.

Even though there was no rain, Prophet Nuh obeyed.

He began building the Ark.

People laughed at him again.

“Why are you building a ship on dry land?” they said.

But Prophet Nuh trusted Allah.

When the Ark was ready, Allah told him:

“Bring your family and pairs of animals onto the Ark.”

Soon… dark clouds filled the sky.

Rain began to fall.

And fall… and fall…

Water rose everywhere.

But inside the Ark, Prophet Nuh and the believers were safe.

The animals were safe.

Allah protected them.

After many days, the rain stopped.

The water slowly went down.

The Ark came to rest on land.

It was a new beginning.

The world was calm again.

And Prophet Nuh thanked Allah.

### Lesson For Kids

Always do what is right, even if others don’t listen.

Be patient and trust Allah.

Allah helps those who stay strong and believe.

### Qur'an Reference

“Indeed, We sent Nuh to his people, saying, ‘Warn your people before a painful punishment comes to them.’”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 71:1`

- `quran_refs`:
  - `21:69`

### Story

Long, long ago, there lived a young boy who loved to think, wonder, and ask questions.

His name was Ibrahim.

Ibrahim looked at the world around him and noticed something strange.

People were worshipping statues.

Some were made of stone…
Some were made of wood…
And some people even made them with their own hands!

Ibrahim wondered,

“How can something we make be our god?”

One night, he looked up at the sky.

He saw a bright star.

“Is this my Lord?” he wondered.

But when the star disappeared, he said,

“No… my Lord would not disappear.”

Then he saw the moon, shining beautifully.

“Is this my Lord?”

But when the moon also went away, he said,

“No… my Lord does not go away.”

Then the sun rose, big and bright.

“Is this my Lord?”

But when the sun set, Ibrahim understood something very important.

“My Lord is the One who created all of this.”

Ibrahim knew the truth.

There is only one God — Allah.

### Story Continued

But his people did not agree.

They became angry when Ibrahim told them to stop worshipping idols.

One day, when no one was around, Ibrahim went to the place where all the idols were kept.

He broke them.

All… except one.

He left the biggest idol standing.

When the people returned, they were shocked.

“Who did this?!” they shouted.

They asked Ibrahim.

And he calmly said,

“Maybe the big one did it. Ask him.”

The people stopped.

They realized something…

The idol could not speak.

It could not move.

It could not help anyone.

For a moment… they understood.

But then they became angry again.

They decided to punish Ibrahim.

They built a huge fire.

The flames were so big and so hot.

They threw Ibrahim into the fire.

But Allah protected him.

Allah said to the fire:

“O fire, be cool and safe for Ibrahim.”

And the fire did not hurt him at all.

Ibrahim came out safe.

Not even a single burn.

Because Allah was with him.

### Ending

Prophet Ibrahim continued to teach people about Allah.

He was brave, truthful, and trusted Allah completely.

And Allah loved him for that.

### Lesson For Kids

Always stand up for the truth.

Even if others disagree.

Allah protects those who believe in Him.

### Qur'an Reference

“Allah said, ‘O fire, be coolness and safety upon Ibrahim.’”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 21:69`

### Story

After many years of waiting, Allah blessed Prophet Ibrahim with a son.

His name was Ismail.

Ibrahim loved his son very, very much.

Ismail was kind, gentle, and obedient. As he grew older, he became a strong and helpful boy.

One day, Prophet Ibrahim had a dream.

But this was not an ordinary dream.

It was a message from Allah.

In the dream, Ibrahim saw himself sacrificing his beloved son, Ismail.

Ibrahim woke up feeling very serious.

He knew this was a test from Allah.

A very big test.

### Story Continued

Ibrahim went to his son and spoke gently:

“My dear son, I saw in a dream that I must sacrifice you. What do you think?”

Ismail did not get angry.

He did not run away.

He trusted Allah.

And he trusted his father.

He said:

“O my father, do as you are commanded. You will find me patient, if Allah wills.”

Ibrahim felt both sad and strong at the same time.

They walked together to the place where the sacrifice would happen.

Step by step…

Side by side…

A father and his son, both trusting Allah.

Ibrahim gently laid Ismail down.

He closed his eyes.

He was ready to do what Allah had asked.

Because he loved Allah more than anything.

### Ending

But then…

Allah called out:

“O Ibrahim! You have fulfilled the vision.”

It was a test.

And they had passed.

Instead of Ismail, Allah sent a ram to be sacrificed.

Ismail was safe.

Ibrahim was relieved.

And Allah was pleased with them both.

This is why, every year during Eid al-Adha, Muslims remember this moment.

A moment of trust.

A moment of obedience.

A moment of love for Allah.

### Lesson For Kids

Trust Allah, even when things feel difficult.

Obeying Allah brings goodness.

Allah always rewards those who are patient and sincere.

### Qur'an Reference

“O my father, do as you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, among the patient.”

### Story

Long ago, there was a young boy named Yusuf.

He was kind, gentle, and very handsome. But what made him special was his pure heart and his love for Allah.

Yusuf had a loving father, Prophet Yaqub, who cared deeply for him.

One night, Yusuf had a dream.

He saw eleven stars, the sun, and the moon all bowing down to him.

He told his father about the dream.

Prophet Yaqub smiled and said,

“My dear son, this dream is special. Allah has a beautiful plan for you.”

### Story Continued

But not everyone felt happy for Yusuf.

His brothers became jealous.

They thought their father loved Yusuf more.

One day, they made a plan.

“Let’s take Yusuf far away,” they said.

They took him to a deep well.

And they left him there… all alone.

It was dark.

It was quiet.

But Yusuf was not afraid.

Because he trusted Allah.

A group of travelers passed by the well.

They found Yusuf and took him with them.

Soon, he was taken to a faraway land and grew up in a new home.

Even though he was far from his family, Yusuf remained honest and kind.

As Yusuf grew older, he faced many difficult tests.

He was treated unfairly.

He was even put in prison, even though he did nothing wrong.

But Yusuf never became angry.

He never forgot Allah.

Even in prison, he helped others and stayed patient.

### Ending

Allah gave Yusuf a special gift.

He could understand dreams.

One day, the king had a confusing dream.

No one could explain it.

But Yusuf could.

He explained the dream wisely, and the king was amazed.

Soon, Yusuf was freed.

And he was given an important position to help the people.

Years later, something amazing happened.

Yusuf’s brothers came to him… but they did not recognize him.

Yusuf knew exactly who they were.

He could have been angry.

He could have punished them.

But instead…

He forgave them.

He chose kindness.

Finally, Yusuf was reunited with his father.

After so many years, they were together again.

And Yusuf’s childhood dream came true.

Everything that happened — the well, the journey, the hardships — was part of Allah’s perfect plan.

### Lesson For Kids

Be patient, even when life is hard.

Never lose hope in Allah.

And always forgive others.

### Qur'an Reference

“Indeed, my Lord is subtle in what He wills. Indeed, it is He who is the Knowing, the Wise.”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 12:100`

## prophet_musa_bedtime_story_v1

- `quran_refs`:
  - `26:63`

### Story

Long, long ago, there was a powerful and cruel king.

His name was Fir‘awn (Pharaoh).

He ruled over Egypt and treated people very badly, especially the Children of Israel.

Fir‘awn was so proud that he even said,

“I am your lord!”

But Allah is the only true Lord.

At that time, a baby boy was born.

His name was Musa.

Fir‘awn had ordered that baby boys be taken away, so Musa’s mother was very worried.

But Allah inspired her:

“Place your baby in a basket and put him in the river. Do not be afraid. I will return him to you.”

It was a very difficult moment.

But she trusted Allah.

She gently placed baby Musa in a basket and set it afloat on the river.

The water carried him away…

### Story Continued

The basket reached the palace of Fir‘awn.

And who found him?

Fir‘awn’s own wife.

She looked at the baby and felt love in her heart.

“Let us keep him,” she said.

And just like that, baby Musa grew up in the palace of the very king who wanted him gone.

Allah protected him.

As Musa grew older, he became strong and brave.

One day, he left the palace and saw something troubling.

A man was being treated unfairly.

Musa tried to help, but things went wrong.

He felt afraid and left the city.

He traveled far and found a quiet place called Madyan.

There, he lived peacefully and later started a family.

Years later, something amazing happened.

While traveling, Musa saw a fire on a mountain.

When he went closer, he heard a voice.

It was Allah speaking to him.

Allah chose Musa to be a prophet.

“Go to Fir‘awn,” Allah commanded, “and tell him to stop his ظلم (wrongdoing).”

Musa felt nervous.

But Allah reassured him:

“I am with you.”

### Ending

Musa went to Fir‘awn with courage.

He showed him signs from Allah.

One of the signs was his staff — when he threw it down, it turned into a snake.

But Fir‘awn refused to believe.

He was too proud.

Allah then commanded Musa to lead his people away.

They left Egypt, but Fir‘awn chased after them with his army.

Soon, they reached a huge sea.

The people were scared.

“There is no escape!” they said.

But Musa said:

“No. Allah is with me. He will guide me.”

Allah commanded Musa to strike the sea with his staff.

And something incredible happened.

The sea split into two!

A path opened in the middle.

Musa and his people walked safely through.

When Fir‘awn and his army followed…

The sea closed.

And they were gone.

Allah saved Musa and his people.

Prophet Musa continued to guide his people, teaching them about Allah and the right way to live.

### Lesson For Kids

Always trust Allah, even in scary situations.

Be brave and stand up against what is wrong.

Allah helps those who believe in Him.

### Qur'an Reference

“Strike the sea with your staff.” And it parted, and each portion was like a great towering mountain.

- `reference`: `Qur’an 26:63`

### Opening Qur'an Reference

“And whoever puts their trust in Allah, then He is sufficient for them.”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 65:3`

### Story

The people of Makkah continued to treat Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his followers badly.

Life became very difficult.

But Allah had a plan.

Allah gave permission for the believers to leave Makkah and travel to a new city called Madinah.

This journey is called the Hijrah.

It was not easy.

They had to leave their homes, their belongings, and everything they knew.

But they trusted Allah.

### Story Continued

One night, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ quietly left Makkah with his close friend, Abu Bakr.

The people who wanted to harm him were searching everywhere.

They even came close...

Very close.

The Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid inside a cave.

Abu Bakr became worried.

But the Prophet ﷺ said gently:

"Do not be sad. Allah is with us."

Allah protected them.

Even when danger was near, they were safe.

After some time, they continued their journey.

And finally...

They reached Madinah.

### Ending

The people of Madinah were very happy.

They welcomed the Prophet ﷺ with love and joy.

This was a new beginning.

In Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ built a community.

He taught people to:

Be kind to one another.

Help the poor.

Be fair and honest.

Pray together.

Live in peace.

He brought people together like one big family.

The Prophet ﷺ was not just a leader.

He was gentle.

He helped others.

He forgave people.

He cared for children, the elderly, and even animals.

Even when he became strong and successful, he never became proud.

He remained humble.

He remained kind.

He remained close to Allah.

### Lesson For Kids

Trust Allah, even when life is hard.

Be kind and helpful to others.

True strength is being gentle and fair.

### Qur'an Reference

“Indeed, Allah is with those who are patient.”

### Opening Qur'an Reference

“And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds.”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 21:107`

### Story

Years passed in Madinah.

The Muslim community grew stronger.

People lived together with kindness, fairness, and faith.

One day, the time came to return to Makkah.

The same city where the Prophet ﷺ had been hurt...

The same place where people were unkind to him...

Now, he returned with strength.

### Story Continued

The people of Makkah were afraid.

They thought:

"What will he do to us now?"

Would he take revenge?

Would he punish them?

But Prophet Muhammad ﷺ showed something greater.

He showed mercy.

He said:

"No blame will there be upon you today. You are all free."

He forgave them.

All of them.

Even those who had been unkind to him.

The people were amazed.

This was not ordinary kindness.

This was the heart of a true prophet.

### Ending

The idols around the Ka'bah were removed.

The truth was restored.

People began to worship Allah alone.

The Prophet ﷺ continued to teach people:

Be honest.

Be kind.

Care for others.

Stay close to Allah.

He reminded them that this life is a journey...

And the best people are those who do good.

Before he passed away, the Prophet ﷺ gave an important message.

He reminded people:

To treat others fairly.

To hold on to the Qur'an.

To stay united.

To remember Allah.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ lived a life full of mercy, patience, and love.

And his message continues in the hearts of millions around the world.

### Lesson For Kids

Forgive others, even when you can take revenge.

Be kind, gentle, and fair.

Follow the teachings of the Prophet ﷺ.

### Qur'an Reference

“There has certainly come to you a Messenger from among yourselves. Grievous to him is what you suffer; he is concerned over you and to the believers is kind and merciful.”

- `reference`: `Qur’an 9:128`

### Closing Reflection For Kids

The Prophet ﷺ showed us how to live:

With kindness.

With patience.

With honesty.

With love for Allah.

And if we follow his example...

We can become better every day.
