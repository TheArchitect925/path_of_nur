# Dua Phase 4 Enhancement Backlog

Date: 2026-03-31

## Potential enhancements

- Add a small in-app source-note treatment for entries that are:
  - companion practice
  - early scholarly practice
  - guidance-note entries with no single fixed short spoken dua
- Consider grouping family-facing duas with secondary helper labels such as:
  - spouse
  - children
  - parents
  - home
  - hosting
- Consider adding a special-days explanation chip for entries like Eid takbir and eclipse guidance so users understand when a wording is widely accepted practice versus a fixed prophetic phrase.
- Run a duplication review across the home/family category because some users may benefit from one canonical family dua rather than multiple close variants for parents and children.
- Add a dataset validator rule that flags:
  - secondary-source entries
  - guidance-note entries with empty Arabic by design
  - duplicate-equivalent meanings across categories

## Risks to review later

- `Eid Greeting` and `Takbir in the First Ten Days of Dhul-Hijjah` are intentionally included as accepted early Muslim / scholarly-practice formulas, not as single fixed prophetic wordings.
- `During an Eclipse` is intentionally a guidance-style entry because the stronger evidence centers on prayer, takbir, dua, charity, and istighfar rather than one short fixed dua formula.
- `For Family Reconciliation` currently uses a Qur'anic hearts-and-forgiveness dua that is broader than family-only conflict and may later benefit from product copy that makes the scope clearer.
